Reporting a text

If you are a rights holder (author, composer, music publisher, collecting society) and do not want a text to be displayed by Lyrikoz, write to nexussautomation@gmail.com with:

  • the title and artist of the song, and if possible the address of a YouTube video where Lyrikoz displays it;
  • what you are asking for (removal, correction);
  • your contact details and the capacity in which you are writing.

Legitimate requests are handled as quickly as possible: the song is added to a list shipped with the extension, and Lyrikoz stops displaying its lyrics from the next version onwards (publishing a version takes a few days, the time of the browser store's review). Because the text itself is stored by LRCLIB and not by Lyrikoz, you may also report it directly to LRCLIB.
